FOLDERHUNT

Your files stay on your PC.

Privacy policy · Updated 21 October 2026 · Version 1.0.1

FolderHunt (previously Local Search Studio) is an independent Windows desktop search application published by Adiityaaa7 and powered by the open-source XERJ engine.

What the app processes

When you choose a folder, the app reads supported files within it to create searchable indexes. The indexes may contain file paths, metadata, and copies of extracted text. Search queries and previews are processed on your computer. Indexes, folder settings, indexing journals, and a local engine authentication key are stored in the app-data location shown in How it works. A small query cache is held in memory for the current session.

No collection by the publisher

The app does not send your files, queries, or indexes to the publisher. This edition contains no publisher analytics, advertising SDK, account sign-in, cloud reranking, or neural-model download. It communicates with its bundled engine through authenticated connections bound to 127.0.0.1 on your PC.

Your choices

You choose which folders to index and can stop indexing. Refresh updates indexed copies after you edit or delete source files. Closing the app stops its engine and clears the in-memory query cache. To remove all saved indexes and settings, close the app and remove the app-data directory shown in How it works. This does not delete your original files. Uninstalling a portable copy alone may leave its app-data directory behind.

Local security

App data is restricted to your Windows user account. The app does not encrypt its indexes. Anyone with access to your Windows account, administrator access, backups, or the storage device may be able to access the data. Index only content you intend to make searchable on this device.

Other services

Microsoft Store handles application distribution and updates under Microsoft's privacy policy. Opening support or source links uses your browser and the destination site's policies. If you voluntarily post a support issue, GitHub processes the information you provide; do not include private documents or credentials in public issues.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will be published with the Store listing. For support and privacy questions, contact the publisher through the app's support page.
